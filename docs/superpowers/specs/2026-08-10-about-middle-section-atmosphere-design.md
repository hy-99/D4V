# About Middle-Section Atmosphere

## Scope

Add restrained visual depth only to the About page mission and officer sections. Do not modify the About hero, high-school invitation, footer, homepage, content, layouts, or interactions.

## Treatment

- Mission: retain the pale-blue foundation, add a translucent cream editorial paper plane, strengthen the existing broad light washes, and place a faint dot field in unused space.
- Officers: use a distinct cooler blue gradient with diffused light at the outer edges and a low-contrast glow behind the dark officer stage.
- Keep every decorative layer behind content and non-interactive.
- Reduce opacity and scale on mobile so decoration does not crowd copy or controls.
- Use no doodles, extra photographs, logos, text, icons, shadows, or animated background effects.

## Validation

- Confirm the two middle sections expose distinct computed background treatments.
- Confirm decorative layers use `pointer-events: none` and stay below content.
- Confirm no horizontal overflow at desktop or mobile sizes.
- Confirm the hero and final high-school section retain their existing selectors and styling.
