# D4V Site Quality Design

## Scope

Preserve the homepage visual design and the approved About and Our Work compositions. Fix shared navigation and accessibility defects, then raise the unfinished routes to the same standard through better content priority, real utility, less repeated imagery, and route-specific layouts.

## Shared Experience

- Make the mobile navigation usable from 390px through 1023px.
- Keep focus inside the open navigation dialog, provide an explicit close control, make the background inert, and restore trigger focus on close.
- Show the current route in desktop and mobile navigation. Workshops and SeniorSafe map to Our Work.
- Give footer links 44px touch targets and send the Report a Scam link to the official FTC reporting service.
- Give every route a specific title and description.
- Use the established landing reveal on every section, shorten the overly long global reveal slightly, and preserve reduced-motion behavior.
- Add an explicit pause/resume control to the About scam carousel without changing its imagery or default rotation.

## Route Improvements

- Resources keeps its field-guide identity but adds a clearly labeled set of official next destinations: FTC scam guidance, ReportFraud.ftc.gov, IdentityTheft.gov, and CISA account-protection guidance.
- Workshops keeps the current real D4V lecture photography and overall composition; only navigation and motion consistency change.
- SeniorSafe becomes a shorter split introduction with one dominant photograph, a compact supporting photo pair, a clear program-purpose statement, and the existing Pause/Ask/Verify sequence. No photograph repeats on the page.
- Get Involved replaces the generic teamwork hero with D4V lecture photography and expands practical participation choices without inventing contact details.
- Get Help places the immediate-danger note before the hero photograph on mobile and adds clear official recovery/reporting destinations after the immediate steps.

## Constraints

- Do not change homepage section layout, copy, imagery, or feature-tile behavior.
- Do not invent email addresses, phone numbers, staff information, statistics, partnerships, or organizational claims.
- Keep all existing local images and dependencies; add no UI or animation library.
- Keep every route semantic, keyboard accessible, responsive, and useful without animation.
